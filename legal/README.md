# Legal Documents

> **DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED.** Nothing in this folder is finalized. These documents were drafted as a starting point to compress attorney time and cost. Do not publish or send to a school district without a New York–licensed attorney with K-12 ed-tech experience reviewing and signing off. The drafts assume Resolution Nation LLC is the contracting entity; replace before review if that changes.

## Files

| File | What it is | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| `privacy-policy.md` | Primary Privacy Policy covering FERPA, COPPA, CCPA, NY Ed Law 2-d. | Parents, schools, regulators |
| `terms-of-service.md` | ToS for end users (students, parents, teachers). School contracts are governed by the separate DPA. | All users |
| `childrens-privacy-notice.md` | Plain-language COPPA-aligned notice for parents of children under 13. | Parents |
| `ny-parent-bill-of-rights.md` | NY Ed Law 2-d §121.3 Parent Bill of Rights template. | NY parents and schools |
| `subprocessors.md` | Current list of vendors that process Resolution Nation data. | Schools (referenced from DPA) |
| `dpa-checklist.md` | Pre-execution checklist for incoming Data Privacy Agreements. | Internal use during sales |

## What's not here

- **Data Processing Agreement (DPA).** Use the Student Data Privacy Consortium's National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) v1r6 as the base — sign up at https://privacy.a4l.org/ndpa/ for the current template. Add the NY Exhibit E (a NYSED-recommended NY-specific addendum). Have your attorney redline it once; reuse for every NY contract.
- **Vendor security questionnaire pre-fills (CASBO, K-12 SIX, NDPA Exhibit E).** Build these from the security evidence pack (Phase 4 deliverable in the roadmap).
- **Cyber liability insurance certificate.** Buy a policy before the first paid contract; attach the COI to outgoing DPAs.

## Maintenance

Update the "Last revised" date at the top of each document every time a material change is made. Most school DPAs require notification of material privacy policy changes; build that into release notes.
