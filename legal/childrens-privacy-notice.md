This is a plain-language summary for parents and guardians of children under 13 who use Resolution Nation. It is not a substitute for our full [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

## What Resolution Nation is

Resolution Nation is an educational app that helps kids set learning goals, work through short "learning workouts," and earn stars they can spend on a digital sticker-style store inside the app. Teachers and parents can see how a child is progressing.

## How a child gets an account

There are two ways:

1. **Through a school.** Most children use Resolution Nation because their teacher signed up the class. In that case, the school is responsible for getting parent consent under federal law (COPPA), and the school is in charge of the child's information. We act as a service provider to the school.

2. **From a family.** If a parent signs their child up directly (without a school), we ask for verifiable parental consent before we create the child's account. Until consent is verified, we do not collect the child's information.

## What we collect from kids

Just what the app needs to work:

- The child's first name and last name (so they appear correctly in class)
- An email address (used only to sign in; we never email children)
- Their grade level (so the app can suggest age-appropriate goals)
- Their answers to in-product learning workouts
- Their star balance and any items they've collected in the in-product store
- Files (like a report card) that a teacher chooses to upload to help personalize the child's learning

## What we do NOT collect

- We do not collect a child's location.
- We do not put advertising in the app, ever.
- We do not let other companies track kids in the app.
- We do not sell or rent any information about kids.
- We do not let anyone outside Resolution Nation talk to the child through the app.

## How we use the information

Only to make the app work for the child and their teacher/parent: showing goals, scoring practice, awarding stars, and letting parents and teachers see progress.

When a teacher uses our optional AI-assisted goal suggestions, we remove personal identifying details before sending data to our AI provider. The AI provider does not train on the data.

## How long we keep it

While the child has an account. If a parent or school asks us to delete the account, we soft-delete it right away and then permanently delete after 30 days.

## What parents can do

You can:

- See what information we have about your child
- Ask us to correct or delete it
- Tell us to stop collecting any more
- Refuse to let your child keep using Resolution Nation

To do any of these, email **privacy@resolutionnation.app**. We will verify you're the parent (or have parental authority) before acting on a request.

If your child uses Resolution Nation through a school and you want to make a request through us, we'll work with the school to honor it — schools control what happens to student records under federal law (FERPA).
