This Privacy Policy describes how Resolution Nation, LLC ("Resolution Nation," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Resolution Nation is an educational technology service designed to help students set learning goals, work through personalized roadmaps, and earn in-product rewards. Our service is primarily provided to K–12 schools under contracts (Data Privacy Agreements, or "DPAs") that designate Resolution Nation as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA"). Where a school has entered into such a contract, the school maintains direct control of student data and Resolution Nation processes that data solely for the educational purposes specified in the contract.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the Service. The categories below are exhaustive — if a category is not listed, we do not collect it.

### a. Account information

- Full name
- Email address
- Role (student, teacher, or parent)
- Grade level (students only, where applicable)
- Avatar (optional, user-selected)
- Authentication identifiers from connected sign-in providers (Google, Apple, Microsoft) — limited to the identifier and email needed to authenticate the user
- A hashed password (only if you choose password authentication)

### b. Educational records (student users)

- Goals you or your teacher set
- Learning roadmaps and steps generated for you
- Responses to in-product workouts (correctness, difficulty, response time)
- Lesson activity, including AI-generated lesson content presented to you and your responses to lesson questions (correctness and progress)
- Writing you submit in the Writing Workshop (short responses, essays, and creative writing), together with AI-generated rubric feedback and scores on that writing, teacher review notes, and any content-moderation flags applied to a submission (see Section 3)
- Voice recordings you make when you use the Read Aloud (reading fluency) feature, together with the text transcript and the reading metrics derived from them (such as words-correct-per-minute and accuracy). These recordings are made only when a student deliberately starts a reading and are used to measure and support reading progress. See Section 3 and Section 6 for how they are used and retained.
- Star balance and inventory items
- Files uploaded by your teacher (report cards, assessment results) that the teacher chooses to associate with your account

### c. Classroom and family relationships

- Membership in classroom "pods," family "pods," and team "pods"
- Parent–child links established through an in-product invite code

### d. Service operations data

- Log entries describing actions taken in the product (audit log) — for security and to satisfy district recordkeeping requirements
- Error reports (with personal information scrubbed) sent to our error monitoring tool
- Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request path, timestamp) retained for a limited period for security and abuse prevention

### e. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect: precise geolocation, advertising identifiers, device contacts, behavioral profiles, or browsing history outside our Service. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in our mobile or web applications.

We capture microphone audio only when a student deliberately starts a reading in the Read Aloud (reading fluency) feature; we never listen in the background or record without an explicit, student-initiated action. The camera is used only when you explicitly take an avatar photo. We do **not** create voiceprints or any biometric identifier from Read Aloud recordings: the audio is used to transcribe the words read and measure reading rate and accuracy, not to identify a person by their voice.

## 3. How we use information

We use information only for the following purposes:

- Operating the Service (showing you your goals, recording your progress, awarding stars, enabling parent and teacher views, processing in-product invite codes).
- Generating AI-assisted learning goal suggestions when initiated by a teacher. Personally identifiable information is stripped before any data leaves our infrastructure for this purpose (see Section 5 — Sub-processors).
- Generating personalized lesson content. Our AI sub-processor receives the skill being practiced, grade band, and the student's current difficulty tier — not the student's name or other direct identifiers — and returns lesson content that we store as part of the student's educational record.
- Providing feedback on student writing. When a student submits writing in the Writing Workshop, the text of the submission is sent to our AI sub-processor (under terms that prohibit training on the content) to produce rubric-based feedback and a suggested score, which the teacher can review, adjust, and approve.
- Keeping students safe through automated content moderation. Student writing submissions are automatically screened for content indicating a safety concern (such as threats, self-harm, or abuse disclosures). Flagged content is routed to the teacher for human review, and an account's writing features may be temporarily restricted pending that review. Moderation exists to protect students; flags and any resulting restrictions are visible to the teacher and, where appropriate, the linked parent. See our Content Moderation & Account Restriction Policy at /legal/content-moderation for details.
- Generating teacher-facing drafts (such as progress notes, IEP goal suggestions, and parent update drafts) when initiated by a teacher, for the teacher to review and edit before use.
- Measuring and supporting reading fluency. When a student uses the Read Aloud feature, the voice recording is sent to a speech-to-text sub-processor (under a data-processing agreement that prohibits using the audio to train its models) to produce a transcript; we then calculate reading metrics and generate supportive feedback for the student. The resulting scores and levels are shown only to the student's teacher and linked parent — not to the student. The audio file itself is stored privately so a teacher or parent can listen back.
- Communicating with you about your account (password resets, security alerts, service announcements relating to your account).
- Security, fraud prevention, and compliance — including responding to lawful requests and enforcing our Terms of Service.
- Improving the Service in aggregate. We do not use student educational records for product improvement except in fully de-identified, aggregated form.

We do not use student personal information for advertising, marketing, or to build behavioral profiles. We do not sell student personal information. We do not share student personal information with third parties for their own use.

## 4. Legal bases — FERPA and COPPA

### a. FERPA (federal)

For students enrolled with schools that have contracted with us, Resolution Nation operates as a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under 34 CFR §99.31(a)(1)(i)(B). The school maintains direct control of student education records; we use those records solely to provide the Service to the school as described in our contract with the school.

### b. COPPA (federal)

The Service is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13. For children under 13:

- When access is provided through a school, we rely on the school to provide consent on behalf of parents for the use of the Service for educational purposes, consistent with FTC guidance for the school-authorized educational use of online services.
- When access is provided directly to a family without a school contract, we obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from the child, and we limit collection to what is necessary to provide the Service.

Parents have the right to review the personal information we have collected from their child, to request deletion, and to refuse further collection. Contact privacy@resolutionnation.app to exercise these rights.

### c. State student-data laws

We comply with state student data privacy laws applicable to our customers, including but not limited to New York Education Law §2-d and Part 121 (see the Parent Bill of Rights at /legal/ny-parent-bill); the Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA) and AB 1584 in California; the Student Online Personal Protection Act (SOPPA) in Illinois; and similar laws in other states.

## 5. Sub-processors

We rely on a limited set of vendors to operate the Service. Each is contractually obligated to protect the data we share with them and to use it only for the purpose for which we engage them. Our current list of sub-processors is maintained at /legal/subprocessors and notified to school customers under our DPAs.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by our contracts with schools, or as required by law.

- Student educational records are retained for the duration of the school's contract with us, plus 30 days for transition, unless the school directs earlier deletion.
- Read Aloud voice recordings are stored in a private, access-controlled location and retained as part of the student's educational record (subject to the same contract and deletion rules above); a school or parent may request deletion of individual recordings at any time. Transcripts and reading metrics are retained with the educational record.
- Audit log entries are retained for two years.
- Server logs are retained for 30 days.
- Account deletion requests trigger soft-deletion immediately and hard-deletion 30 days later. After hard-deletion, backups age out per our retention schedule (currently 30 days of point-in-time recovery on our primary database).

Schools and parents may request deletion at any time through their respective channels.

## 7. Security

We protect personal information using:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher).
- Encryption at rest on our primary database.
- Row-level security policies that restrict every database query to the records the requesting user is authorized to see.
- Multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts.
- Audit logging of sensitive actions.
- A documented incident response plan with a 7-day breach notification standard for New York customers (per Ed Law 2-d) and applicable timelines for other jurisdictions.
- Vulnerability scanning, dependency monitoring, and periodic penetration testing.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting your information, we will notify you and affected schools as required by applicable law and our contracts.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and your role:

- **Parents** may review, correct, or request deletion of their child's personal information. For students enrolled through a school, requests are typically directed to the school; we will support the school's response.
- **Students 18 or older** have the same rights as parents with respect to their own information.
- **California residents** have rights under the CCPA/CPRA including access, deletion, correction, and opt-out of "sale" or "sharing" (we do neither).
- **EU/UK residents** have rights under GDPR/UK-GDPR. We are not actively marketing the Service to EU/UK users at this time; if that changes, this Policy will be updated.

To exercise any right, contact privacy@resolutionnation.app. We will verify your identity (and your relationship to a student, if applicable) before acting on the request.

## 9. International data transfers

We process data in the United States. If we transfer data internationally in the future, we will update this Policy and adopt appropriate safeguards.
